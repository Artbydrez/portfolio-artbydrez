## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The site is genuinely authored for this product, not a reskinned template — real project taxonomy, real case-study detail per project, a real bio timeline, real contact handles. The horizontal-slider interaction and magazine-style grids are a considered choice that itself demonstrates craft. Specificity collapses exactly at the highest-stakes screen: Contact and every "talk to me" CTA are written in generic freelance-agency voice ("Parlons de votre projet," "Travailler ensemble," "Disponible") — copy that could sit on any dark portfolio template, on the one page PRODUCT.md says must speak to a recruiter deciding to hire an employee, not a client commissioning work.

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI scan ran in degraded/regex-only mode (HTML parser modules unavailable) and surfaced only 3 hits, one a likely false positive (`overused-font` fired on the bare `font-family:` declaration for Helvetica Neue — not one of the fonts the rule actually targets). It also flagged the numbered "01 — Identité visuelle" style kickers as a slop pattern (15 instances on one page) — here that reads as a deliberate, consistently-applied design device rather than filler, so treat that specific hit as a stylistic false positive, not a defect.

## Overall Impression

The visual craft and IA are ahead of the site's copy and interaction robustness. A recruiter skimming quickly would likely be impressed by the work shown, then hit friction the moment they try to act on it: Back doesn't work, the CTA reads like a freelance pitch, and three of the four flagship projects don't disclose they're training exercises. The single biggest opportunity is closing that gap between "the portfolio looks hireable" and "the site behaves and speaks like it's talking to a recruiter."

## What's Working

1. **Portfolio drill-down IA** (overview → category grid → detail) matches a time-pressed recruiter's real need — sample breadth first, go deep on demand — and serves the "polyvalence" positioning without diluting any one discipline.
2. **Visual/typographic restraint** — one dark theme, one mint accent, one type scale. For a graphic designer's own portfolio this reads as confident, not generic-safe, and Aesthetic/Minimalist scored a clean 4/4.
3. **Specific personality in the copy** ("version_finale_2_V26", "Sans ça, ta marque sort en pyjama") lands the "rigoureux mais auto-dérision" tone from PRODUCT.md without tipping into unprofessional filler.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Browser Back button is broken across the entire site**
- Why it matters: `goTo()` sets `location.hash` but nothing listens for `hashchange`. Verified live: Home → Portfolio → Identité → Dwello, then Back leaves the URL at `#2` while the visible page stays frozen on the Dwello detail slide. Back is the single most reflexive gesture for someone comparing several portfolios quickly — a frozen page reads as broken and is a plausible instant-close moment.
- Fix: add a `hashchange` listener that calls `goTo()` with the new hash value when it differs from the current page, and make sure `goTo()` doesn't fight it by re-pushing history.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Contact and CTA copy speak "client," the audience is "recruiter"**
- Why it matters: "Parlons de votre projet," "Décrivez votre projet en quelques lignes," "Travailler ensemble →," and the nav pill "Disponible" are all freelance-pitch phrasing. PRODUCT.md is explicit that this site's job is to get a recruiter to hire Loris as an employee, not to win a freelance brief — this is exactly the mismatch the brief warns about, on the one page that has to convert.
- Fix: reframe around hiring — e.g. "Vous recrutez ?", "Discutons du poste," "Votre message" instead of "Votre projet" — optionally keep a small secondary line for freelance/collab inquiries if that door should stay open.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Body paragraphs bleed thousands of pixels off-screen**
- Why it matters: the live-DOM detector caught multiple `<p>` elements rendering with a right edge as far as -5743px off the visible viewport — a real layout bug, not a style nitpick. Content in that state is either invisible, causing horizontal scroll, or both, depending on the container.
- Fix: needs a quick look to pin down which template/section produces this (likely a fixed-width or non-wrapping container feeding a long paragraph) and add proper `max-width`/`overflow-wrap`.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Primary navigation is unreachable by keyboard or screen reader**
- Why it matters: `.portfolio-card`, `.carousel-slide`, `.po-panel`, and the dropdown's `<a>` tags (no `href`) all rely on bare `onclick` with no `tabindex`, `role`, or keydown handling — confirmed by source inspection. That means every portfolio thumbnail, all 4 home-carousel slides, all 4 portfolio-overview panels, and the Portfolio submenu's links are unreachable without a mouse. This compounds with the detector's independent finding of low contrast (3.4:1, 2.6:1 vs. the 4.5:1 requirement) and sub-11px text on labels inside these same components — a keyboard/screen-reader-dependent recruiter cannot open a single project, and would struggle to read the labels even if they could.
- Fix: convert these to real `<button>`/`<a href>` elements (already done correctly for nav-link, hamburger, and theme-toggle elsewhere in the same file) or add `role`, `tabindex="0"`, and Enter/Space handling; bump `--text-muted`/`--text-faint` a few steps lighter to clear 4.5:1.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] 3 of 4 case studies never disclose they're training exercises**
- Why it matters: PRODUCT.md is explicit and repeated — Dwello, FOMO, SPARK, and Nebula FC are all fictional school/personal exercises, and the site must never imply otherwise. Only Nebula FC carries "fictif" / "Travail de fin d'étude" framing; Dwello, FOMO, and SPARK present full client-style briefs with Year/Category/Tools metadata identical in form to real commissioned work. A recruiter has no signal that 3 of 4 headline projects are training pieces, which risks a credibility gap if it surfaces in an interview instead of on the page.
- Fix: reuse the existing `project-meta-item` slot pattern to add a consistent "Projet école" / "Exercice de formation" value to Dwello, FOMO, and SPARK, framed as deliberate skill-building — matching the transparency Nebula FC already has.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (keyboard/screen-reader-dependent recruiter)**: Tabbing through Home reaches the nav pill fine, but none of the 4 carousel slides are in the tab order, so Sam can't reach a single project category from the homepage without a mouse. On Portfolio Overview, all 4 `.po-panel` divs are skipped by Tab entirely — Identité/Sport/Motion/TFE are all unopenable. The dropdown's category links have no `href`, so they're excluded from the tab order too. Net effect: Sam can reach Contact and About, but may never open a single project — disqualifying for a site whose entire value proposition is "look at my work."

**Riley (stress-tester)**: Reproduced the Back-button freeze directly (Home → Portfolio → Identité → Dwello → Back leaves the URL at `#2`, page frozen on Dwello). Under a throttled connection, the Motion-design panel's background video can abort mid-load, leaving a plain black rectangle with only "03 — MOTION DESIGN" as text — no fallback image, so the one panel meant to represent an entire discipline can show nothing.

**Casey (distracted mobile user)**: At 390×844, the fixed Instagram icon (bottom:24px, right:20px) sits visually on top of the "Envoyer le message →" submit button on Contact — a thumb aiming to send is equally likely to hit Instagram and leave the site at the one moment it exists to trigger. The home hero's dead vertical zone (see Minor Observations) is worse on mobile, giving Casey an extra beat to bounce before reaching the pitch and CTA.

## Minor Observations

- Home hero (`.home-left`, `justify-content: space-between`) leaves a large empty gap between the name block and the pitch+CTA at common viewport heights (~900px desktop, 844px mobile) — works against the "understand the profile in seconds" principle.
- No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling anywhere: the eyebrow-word cycle and the 5s auto-advancing home carousel run indefinitely regardless of OS motion settings.
- Semantic gap: `<h1>` is followed directly by an `<h3>` on project pages with no `<h2>` in between.
- The status-dot "Disponible" indicator pulses infinitely — same reduced-motion gap as above, minor but easy to bundle with that fix.
- The "Message préparé ✓" success state fires even though `mailto:` may silently fail to send on webmail-only setups (a limitation PRODUCT.md itself already notes) — consider softening the copy or adding a copy-to-clipboard fallback.
- Leftover dead CSS/JS for a removed home "disc-strip" band from the ticker-removal commit — harmless, worth a cleanup pass sometime.
